Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

SwaraLab is a singer studio for Android, published as com.swaralab.app.

Data that stays on your phone

SwaraLab does not collect personal data for the developer and does not send sessions, takes, or search text to a SwaraLab server.

Sessions, dry takes, imported tracks, mixes you export, and any name or email you save stay in the app’s private storage on that phone. The developer cannot see them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them from the phone.

A version of the app may let you save a name, an email address, and a password on the phone. The password is stored only as a hash. That account is not uploaded, and it is not required to use the studio.

The app may write a short on-device activity log (for example that a screen opened, or that a search happened and how long the query was). That log stays in the app’s files. It is not a cloud analytics product.

Microphone, camera, and files

This version asks for the microphone to record your voice, optional camera for a vertical video take, and audio-file access so you can pick a song already on the phone. You can refuse. A document picker can still choose one file without broad storage access.

Recordings are 48 kHz dry WAV files in the app folder. Headphone effects you hear while singing are not written into that dry file. Mix & Save writes a separate mix you choose to keep or share.

While you are recording, Android may show a sticky notification so the take can continue if the screen locks or you briefly leave the app.

Sharing

If you share a take or a mix, SwaraLab opens the Android share sheet and grants a temporary read URI to the file. Nothing is posted to a SwaraLab feed. WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, or any other app you pick then applies its own policy to the file you send.

A duet invite is a zip you send the same way. The friend opens it in SwaraLab on their phone. It is not a public post.

What this app does not do

The app does not show ads. It does not sell data. It does not create an account on a SwaraLab server. It does not upload vocals for lyric alignment or scoring. It does not include a store of commercial songs.

On-device lyric follow during a take uses voice activity only. Speech recognition is not started while the microphone is recording, so the dry take is not interrupted and audio is not sent to a speech service.

Children

SwaraLab is not directed at children. It is meant for people aged 18 and over.
