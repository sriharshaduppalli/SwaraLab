# SwaraLab privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

SwaraLab is a singer studio for Android, published as `com.swaralab.app`.

## Data that stays on your phone

SwaraLab does not collect personal data and does not send it to the developer or to any other company.

Sessions, takes, and tracks you import stay in the app’s private storage on that phone. The developer cannot see them. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them from the phone.

A version of the app may let you save a name, an email address, and a password on the phone. The password is stored only as a hash. That account is not uploaded, and it is not required to use the studio.

## What this version does not do

The app does not show ads. It does not sell data. It does not use analytics. It does not ask you to create an account on a server. The build currently on Google Play does not use the microphone or the camera, and it does not export a finished recording.

If a later version records audio or video, it will ask for permission first, and the recording will stay on the phone. If that version lets you share a file, the share goes through an app you choose, such as WhatsApp or Instagram. That app’s own policy then applies to the file you send.

## Children

SwaraLab is not directed at children. It is meant for people aged 18 and over.
